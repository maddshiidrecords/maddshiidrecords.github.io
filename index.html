About Madd’Shiid Records

Madd’Shiid Records is an independent music collective based in Kroonstad, South Africa, shaping hip-hop, trap, R&B and alternative sound.

Built on innovation, audio engineering and coding, the collective represents a new generation of artists creating without limits, from the first beat to the final video.
